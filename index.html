Transparency, privacy and affiliate disclosure

This is an independent planning tool. Listings do not imply partnerships or endorsements. No exclusive discounts are claimed without a verified agreement. Outbound links may eventually be affiliate links; paid placements will be labeled. Trips and founder-preview counts are saved in your browser. If the operator configures optional site-wide analytics, anonymous listing IDs and click/add event types are also recorded; no account, booking, or payment information is collected. Shared itinerary links encode trip details in the URL, so anyone with the link can view them. Do not include sensitive information. Venue photos are not reproduced without permission.
