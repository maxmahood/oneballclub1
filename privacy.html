Privacy information

This site stores itinerary details and local demo counts in your browser's local storage. You can clear this data using your browser's site-data controls.

Shared trip links contain itinerary details, including notes, inside the link. Anyone with that link can read them. Do not add personal or sensitive information.

If optional analytics are configured, the website sends anonymous listing identifiers and outbound-click or trip-add event types to the site's server, where event timestamps are stored. No names, email addresses, or booking confirmations are requested for this feature. The hosting provider may process technical request data such as IP addresses under its own policies.

Third-party booking sites have their own privacy policies. One Ball Club does not complete bookings or collect payment details.

Business relationships, paid listings and affiliate links will be disclosed where applicable. No discounts are guaranteed.

Draft disclosure: operator should add a contact email and review applicable privacy obligations before promotion.
